How it works

  1. Forward your school emails once.

    One simple Gmail rule, and every school email comes to School Night. No app to download.

  2. We keep only what matters.

    Uniform days, trips, forms, money, early pickups. Newsletters and class chatter stay out of your way.

  3. The night before and the morning of.

    You and your nanny get a WhatsApp the evening before and again in the morning, at the times you choose. We suggest 8pm and 6am.
